Venturi Flow Meter: 06-261

CAD design and COMSOL simulation for a 3D printed venturi flow meter, built by a four-person team around an enlarged artery, with blood flow simulated by corn syrup in water at different concentrations.

What did I do?

  • Completed all renditions of the CAD model for the flow meter geometry.
  • Debugged segments of the COMSOL simulation.

Why does this work?

Constricting the flow through a narrow throat speeds the liquid up. By Bernoulli's principle that faster flow has lower pressure, so the pressure difference across the throat gives the velocity.
