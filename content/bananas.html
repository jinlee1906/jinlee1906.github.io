Bananas Bananas Bananas!

For our final project in 09-221: Analytical Chemistry Lab, three classmates and I measured the potassium in unripe bananas, ripe bananas, and plantains to find the best snack for pre-exam jitters.

  • We dehydrated, ashed, and acid-digested each fruit, then quantified potassium by ion-precipitate back titration and by atomic absorption spectroscopy (AAS).
  • Titration was the most accurate, AAS the most precise (R² > 0.995), and standard addition the most suitable because it revealed a strong matrix effect.
  • Plantains came out on top with the most potassium.
