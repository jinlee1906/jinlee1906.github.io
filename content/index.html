About

Hello,

I'm Jin, a junior studying Chemical Engineering at Carnegie Mellon University. I'm drawn to the places where chemistry turns into hardware, especially battery design and soft robotics.

My path here ran through dual enrollment at Georgia State and Emory, then biochemical engineering coursework at the University of Georgia, before I transferred to CMU.

This past summer I interned with GP EnergyTech in Hong Kong and China, building and testing nickel-zinc cells and helping run a factory floor of 500+ people. This October I'm joining Dr. Carmel Majidi's Soft Machines Lab to research gecko-inspired padding for soft aquatic robots.

Outside of class I lead the Singapore Students Association as president, lift, make perfume (I'm working toward launching my own brand, REN Perfumes), cook some really good meal preps, and am slowly learning bass guitar.

Want to talk batteries, robots, or fragrance? Reach me at jinlee2@andrew.cmu.edu.
