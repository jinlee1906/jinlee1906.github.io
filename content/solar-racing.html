Carnegie Mellon Solar Racing

Subteam: Power
Status: Active

Carnegie Mellon Solar Racing is building a solar-electric racing boat. I'm on the power subteam, which handles how energy gets from the sun to the propeller: solar array, batteries, motor controller, motor.

New members start small, measuring two 0.45 W solar panels to see how open-circuit voltage, short-circuit current, the maximum power point, series and parallel wiring, shading, and panel angle shape a panel's output.

The same physics scales up to the boat: strings of panels, an MPPT that keeps the array near its maximum power point, and the wiring, waterproofing, and telemetry a full-size system needs.
