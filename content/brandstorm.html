L'Oréal Brandstorm

Concept and case study for a wearable perfume watch. This project explores product form, user interaction, and scent delivery mechanisms.

Overview

In a team of three, we designed Emoi, a wearable bionic watch that tracks heart rate and blood pressure through microsensors. When it detects signs of an anxiety attack, the watch dispenses a specially formulated perfume shown to modulate GABA-A receptors, the brain's main calming (inhibitory) receptors.

Role

I designed the watch's chassis (shown here) and created a preliminary formula for the calming fragrance using linalool and linalyl acetate. My teammates developed the user experience and integrated the piezo disks and microcontrollers into the chassis.
