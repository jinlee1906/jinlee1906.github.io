Venturi Flow Meter: 06-261

Summary
CAD design and COMSOL simulation for a 3D printed venturi flow meter, completed as part of a group project (4 members). We designed a flow meter based around an enlarged artery and simulated blood flow using different concentrations of corn syrup in water.

What did I do?

  • Completed all renditions of the CAD model for the flow meter geometry.
  • Debugged segments of the COMSOL simulation.

Why does this work?

The Venturi flow meter works by constricting the fluid flow through a narrow segment, which increases the velocity of the liquid. As Bernoulli's Principle states, the increase in speed causes a decrease in pressure, allowing velocity to be calculated by comparing pressure differences.
