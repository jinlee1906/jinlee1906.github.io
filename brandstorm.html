L'oreal Brandstorm

Concept and case study for a wearable perfume watch. This project explores product form, user interaction, and scent delivery mechanisms.

Overview

Designed a compact wearable device that dispenses controlled micro-doses of perfume. Explored materials, user scenarios, and packaging.

Role

Concept design, user scenarios, and prototyping of the scent delivery mechanism.
