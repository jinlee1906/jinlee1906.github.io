CMU SSA

Organization: Singapore Students Association

Role: President

Status: Active

The Singapore Students Association at Carnegie Mellon creates a vibrant and inclusive community for students of Singaporean heritage and allies. Through cultural events, discussions, and social gatherings, SSA fosters a sense of belonging and celebrates diversity on campus. As President of the organization, I act as the primary liasion between the University officials, organization members, external partners (Deutsche Bank, HSBC, Red Dot Link), and the Singapore Government.
